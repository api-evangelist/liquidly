# Liquidly

> Liquidly (Liquidly, Inc.) is a New York based financial technology company building infrastructure for illiquid assets — a permission-based secondary marketplace that lets holders of private fund interests transact without disrupting the fund. Broker-dealer services are offered through Liquidly IAS, LLC, registered with FINRA and a member of SIPC.

Liquidly serves three sides of a private-markets secondary transaction:

- **Fund managers** — a streamlined, centrally supervised transfer process, market data, and the flexibility to structure longer lock-up vehicles.
- **Seller investors** — a manager-approved selling process, access to a network of prospective buyers, and standardized commercial terms.
- **Buyer investors** — manager-granted asset permissions, standardized diligence materials, and technology-enabled execution.

## APIs

Liquidly publishes **no public API**, developer portal, SDK, CLI, or technical documentation as of 2026-07-19. The product is reached only through an authenticated web application at https://demo.liquidly.com/Account/Login. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, webhook catalog, MCP server, or agent-facing surface to describe.
